How it works

Sign a contract with your future self.

  1. 01

    Describe the task

    Title, description, total time, total commitment. Anything vague gets flagged before you even hit submit.

  2. 02

    AI splits it up

    GPT breaks it into 3–8 sequential, verifiable steps with a per-step time budget and credit slice.

  3. 03

    Edit, regenerate, balance

    Tweak any step, ask the model to retry it, or auto-balance amounts to match your total — exactly to the cent.

  4. 04

    Charge & execute

    Lock the commitment with a (mock) bank charge. Each step has a hard timer with up to 3 × 30% extensions.

Rules of the contract

If a step times out, all remaining credit is forfeit.

  • Completed steps keep their credit.
  • Failed step + all later steps are forfeited.
  • You can extend a live step up to 3 times — each adds 30% of the original budget.
